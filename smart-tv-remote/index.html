Privacy Policy — Smart TV Remote

Last updated: November 10, 2026 · Developer: AT Digital Studio · Contact: contact@atdigitalstudio.com

This policy explains how the Android app Smart TV Remote (com.smart.tv.remote.control, "the App") handles information.

In short: the App talks directly to your TV on your local Wi-Fi network. Your TV, your remote-control commands, the text you type and the media you cast are never sent to us. The App does not require an account.

1. Finding and controlling your TV

To find TVs, the App searches your local network using standard protocols (mDNS/NSD, SSDP/UPnP). This reveals the names, brands, models and local IP addresses of devices on your network to the App. Remote-control commands (keys, touchpad movements, app launches) and the text you type with the keyboard feature are sent only to the TV you selected, over your local network. The App reads your Wi-Fi connection state to show whether you are connected.

2. Saved TV and pairing

So that your TV stays paired like a real remote, the App stores on your device: the TV's name, brand, model, local IP address, protocol, the date of the last connection, the TV's network (MAC) address and the pairing credential issued by the TV (Samsung token, LG client key or Android TV certificate). This information never leaves your device except to talk to your TV, is not sent to us and is excluded from Android cloud backup. "Forget TV" in Settings deletes it, as does clearing the App's data or uninstalling it.

3. Turning the TV on (Wake-on-LAN)

When you press the power key while a saved TV is off, the App broadcasts a standard "Wake-on-LAN" packet containing the TV's MAC address on your local network. It is never sent over the internet.

4. Casting photos and videos

Photos and videos are opened only when you pick them with the Android picker; the App does not ask for access to your whole gallery. A picked file is served by a small temporary server inside the App, reachable only on your local network, so the TV can play it. Files are not copied, uploaded or sent to us. Casting uses the Google Cast framework provided by Google, which may process technical data needed to operate Cast according to Google's privacy policy.

5. Settings stored on your device

Your preferences (haptic feedback, the end date of an ad-free period, whether the introduction was shown, your Remove Ads status) and your chosen language are stored locally. They are not sent to us.

6. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads through Google AdMob: banners, native ads (always labelled "Ad"), full-screen ads, app open ads and an optional rewarded video (watched only if you choose to, in exchange for 24 hours without ads). To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process the device advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP, device and app information, and ad interaction data.

Where required (for example in the EEA, UK, Switzerland and certain US states), the App asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy settings (shown when applicable). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. Users who bought Remove Ads see no ads.

Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

7. Purchases, updates and reviews

The Remove Ads purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. We never receive your payment details; the App only receives confirmation of the purchase. Update checks use Google Play's In-App Updates service. When you rate the App, you are sent to Google Play and the review is handled under your Google account.

8. Contacting us

If you write to us, we receive only what you choose to send (your e-mail address and message) and use it only to answer you.

9. Permissions

  • Internet, network and Wi-Fi state, Wi-Fi multicast: find and control TVs on your local network, cast media, load ads.
  • Nearby Wi-Fi devices (Android 13+, declared "never for location"): discover TVs on your network. The App never derives your location from it.
  • Vibration: haptic feedback on key presses (can be turned off).
  • Advertising ID: ads, as described in section 6.

10. Data sharing

We do not sell personal data. The App collects no personal data on our servers. Data is processed by Google only to provide advertising, casting, purchases and updates.

11. Data retention

Local data stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App. Data collected by Google is retained according to Google's policies. E-mails are kept only as long as needed to handle your request.

12. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

13. Security

Data in transit to Google services is encrypted (HTTPS). Communication with the TV stays on your local network; some TV protocols are not encrypted, so use the App on networks you trust.

14. Your rights

Depending on your location you may have rights to access, correct or delete data. For data held by Google, use Google's tools. For any question, contact contact@atdigitalstudio.com.

15. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date shows the latest version.
