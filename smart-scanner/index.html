Privacy Policy — Smart Scanner: PDF & OCR

Last updated: November 2, 2026 · Developer: AT Digital Studio · Contact: contact@atdigitalstudio.com

This policy explains how the Android app Smart Scanner: PDF & OCR (com.smartscanner.documents, "the App") handles information.

1. Your documents stay on your device

Scans, PDFs, recognized text, signatures, folders and settings are stored only in the App's private storage on your device. They are never uploaded to us or to any server, and we have no access to them.

The App excludes this data from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so your documents are not copied to your Google account backup.

When you share or export a document, it goes only to the app or location you choose (for example a messaging app, e-mail or a folder on your device).

You can delete documents at any time. Deleted documents stay in "Recently deleted" for 30 days and are then erased automatically. Settings → Delete all data erases every document, folder and signature immediately. Uninstalling the App also removes all of its data.

2. Camera and files

The camera is used only to scan documents and QR codes / barcodes. Images are processed on your device. The App does not record video or audio.

The App does not request access to your photo library or storage. When you import an image or a PDF, you pick it with the Android system picker, and the App only receives the files you select.

Contacts: when you choose "Save contact" on a business card, the App opens your contacts app with the details filled in. The App does not read your contacts.

3. Text recognition and code scanning (Google ML Kit)

Text recognition (OCR) and QR code / barcode scanning run on your device using Google ML Kit, provided through Google Play services. Your images and recognized text are not sent to us. Google Play services may download or update the recognition models and may send Google technical information about the performance and use of these features, as described by Google: https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process the device advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP, device and app information, and ad interaction data. Ads never receive the content of your documents.

Where required (for example in the EEA, UK, Switzerland and certain US states), the App asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices (shown when applicable). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

5. Google Play features

The App uses Google Play In-App Review (to let you rate the App) and Google Play In-App Updates (to tell you when a new version is available). These features are provided by Google Play and are subject to Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

6. No account, no analytics

The App does not require an account and does not ask for your name or e-mail. It does not use analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.

7. Data sharing

We do not sell personal data. We do not collect your documents. The only data processed by a third party is the advertising data and the Google Play / ML Kit technical data described above, processed by Google.

8. Data retention

Documents stay on your device until you delete them or uninstall the App. Data collected by Google is retained according to Google's policies.

9. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

10. Security

Documents are kept in the App's private storage, which other apps cannot access. When a document is permanently deleted, its files are overwritten before removal. Data in transit to Google services is encrypted (HTTPS).

11. Your rights

Depending on your location you may have rights to access, correct or delete data. Your documents are fully under your control on your device. For data held by Google, use Google's tools. For any question, contact contact@atdigitalstudio.com.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date shows the latest version.
