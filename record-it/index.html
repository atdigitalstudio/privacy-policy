Privacy Policy — Record It: Screen Recorder

Last updated: November 18, 2026 · Developer: AT Digital Studio · Contact: contact@atdigitalstudio.com

This policy explains how the Android app Record It: Screen Recorder (com.recordit.screenrecorder, "the App") handles information. The App is designed so that your recordings never leave your device unless you choose to share them.

1. Your recordings stay on your device

Screen recordings, microphone audio, internal app audio, face-cam video, edited videos, saved frames, drafts and any music you add are created and stored only on your device (in Movies/RecordIt and Pictures/RecordIt, or in the App's private storage for editor drafts). We do not upload, collect, view or store them on any server.

A file only leaves your device when you share it yourself through the Android share menu, and it is then handled by the app you choose.

2. Permissions and why they are used

  • Screen capture (MediaProjection): records your screen, only after you tap Record and approve the Android system prompt each time.
  • Microphone: records your voice or, on Android 10 and later, the audio of apps that allow capture, only while you are recording and only if you choose an audio mode. It is also used for voice-over in the editor when you start it.
  • Camera: shows the optional face-cam bubble, only when you turn it on.
  • Foreground service and notifications: keep the recording running and show a notification with Pause and Stop controls.
  • Display over other apps: shows the optional floating recording controls, only if you enable them.
  • Storage (Android 8 and 9 only): saves videos to your gallery on older Android versions.
  • Internet and network state: used only to load advertisements.

You can deny any permission. The App keeps working with the features that do not need it (for example, recording without audio).

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and shows ads through Google AdMob (banner, native, interstitial, app open and optional rewarded ads). To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process the device advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP, device and app information, ad interaction data (views and taps) and crash diagnostics. Ads never receive your recordings or their content.

Full-screen ads are never shown while you are recording or exporting a video. Rewarded ads are always optional: you choose to watch one to unlock Maximum export quality for the current session.

Where required (for example in the EEA, UK, Switzerland and certain US states), the App asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in Android settings.

Google's policies: https://policies.google.com/privacy · https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

4. Google Play In-App Updates

The App uses Google Play In-App Updates to tell you when a new version is available and to download it if you choose. This feature is provided by Google Play and is subject to Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

5. No account, no analytics

We do not run servers for this App and do not collect personal information such as your name, e-mail address, contacts or files. The App has no user accounts and does not use third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. If you e-mail us, we use your message only to answer you.

6. Data sharing

We do not sell personal data. The only data processed by a third party is the advertising and Google Play data processed by Google (sections 3 and 4).

7. Data retention and deletion

Your recordings remain on your device until you delete them, from the App or your gallery. Uninstalling the App removes its private data, including editor drafts. Data collected by Google is retained according to Google's policies; you can limit it by resetting or deleting your advertising ID.

8. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

9. Security

Recordings are stored with Android's standard storage, and drafts in the App's private storage, which other apps cannot access. Data sent by the advertising SDK is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

10. Your rights

Depending on your location you may have rights to access, correct or delete data. Your recordings are fully under your control on your device. For data held by Google, use Google's tools. For any question, contact contact@atdigitalstudio.com.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date shows the latest version, always available on this page.
