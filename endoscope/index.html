Privacy Policy — Endoscope Camera: USB OTG Inspection

Last updated: November 23, 2026 · Developer: AT Digital Studio · Contact: contact@atdigitalstudio.com

This policy explains how the Android app Endoscope Camera: USB OTG Inspection (com.tah.endoscope, "the App") handles information.

In short: the App shows the live image of a USB endoscope / borescope camera connected to your phone, and lets you take photos and record videos. Your photos and videos stay on your phone. The App has no account, no server of its own and does not upload your images. The only data collected off your device is the data processed by Google to show ads and run Google Play features, as described below.

1. USB camera, photos and videos

The live image comes from the external USB (UVC) camera you plug into your phone. Photos (JPG) and videos (MP4) are created only when you press the capture or record button, and are saved on your device in Pictures/Endoscope and Movies/Endoscope. They are never uploaded by the App. You can view, share or delete them in the App's gallery or in any gallery app. When you choose "Share", Android's share sheet sends the selected files to the app you pick; we do not see them.

If you turn on "Record sound", the phone microphone is used only while a video is being recorded, and the sound is stored only inside that video file.

2. OTG checker

The OTG checker reads technical information that Android exposes about USB: whether USB host (OTG) is supported, and the name, vendor ID, product ID and class of connected USB devices. This information is shown on screen only. It is not stored and not sent to us.

3. Data stored on your device

The App stores a few local settings: whether you completed the introduction, your chosen language, capture options (sound, default resolution, shutter sound), the last camera resolution used, a counter of successful saves used to ask for a rating at most once every 30 days, the number of app sessions and the end time of an "ad-free" period you may unlock. These settings stay on your device and may be included in your Android device backup if backup is enabled. You can delete them by clearing the App's data or uninstalling it.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and shows ads through Google AdMob: banners, native ads, full-screen (interstitial) ads, app open ads and optional rewarded ads ("watch an ad for 30 minutes without pop-up ads"). Ads are never shown over the live camera view or during a recording. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process the device advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP, device and app information, and ad interaction data.

Where required (for example in the EEA, UK, Switzerland and certain US states), the App asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options (shown when applicable). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

5. Google Play features

The App uses Google Play In-App Review to let you rate the App, and Google Play In-App Updates to tell you when a new version is available and install it. Both are handled by Google Play under your Google account; we do not receive your account details.

6. Permissions

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA): required by Android (9 and later) to open an external USB video camera. The App never uses your phone's built-in cameras.
  • Microphone (android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO): optional, asked only when you turn on sound for videos; used only while recording.
  • Storage (WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE and READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, Android 9 and earlier only): save and list your photos and videos. On Android 10 and later no storage permission is used.
  • USB host feature: communicate with the connected USB camera (Android asks you to allow USB access for each camera).
  • Internet and network state (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): ads, consent form and Google Play features.
  • Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS): added by the Google Mobile Ads SDK for ads, as described in section 4.
  • Wake lock and foreground service (WAKE_LOCK, FOREGROUND_SERVICE): added by the USB camera and Google libraries for short background tasks; the App does not run a foreground service of its own.

The App does not request access to your location, contacts, call logs, or to photos and videos created by other apps.

7. Data sharing

We do not sell personal data. Data is processed by Google only to provide advertising, consent management, in-app reviews and in-app updates. We do not use any other analytics or tracking service.

8. Data retention

Photos, videos and settings stay on your device until you delete them or uninstall the App (photos and videos remain in your gallery after uninstalling unless you delete them). Data collected by Google is retained according to Google's policies. E-mails you send us are kept only as long as needed to answer you.

9. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

10. Security

Communication with Google services is encrypted (HTTPS). The App itself does not send your personal information or your images to any server.

11. Your rights

Depending on your location you may have rights to access, correct or delete data. For data held by Google, use Google's tools. For any question, contact contact@atdigitalstudio.com.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date shows the latest version.
