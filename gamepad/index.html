Privacy Policy — GamePad: Smart Controller

Last updated: October 28, 2026 · Developer: AT Digital Studio · Contact: contact@atdigitalstudio.com

This policy explains how the Android app GamePad: Smart Controller (com.gamepad.smartcontroller, "the App") and its companion computer app GamePad Link handle information.

1. Data stored on your device

Controller profiles, custom button layouts, recently used games and settings are stored locally on your device. They are not sent to us. Android may include them in your device backup (Google account backup) according to your device settings. You can delete them by clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App.

The App does not require an account and does not ask for your name, e-mail, contacts, location, photos or files.

2. Bluetooth, Wi-Fi/USB connection and motion sensors

Bluetooth: the App uses Bluetooth permissions only to appear as a game controller and connect to the devices you choose (PC, console, TV, tablet). Controller inputs are sent directly to that device and are not sent to us.

GamePad Link (Wi-Fi or USB): when you use GamePad Link, the App opens a connection on your local network or over the USB cable to send controller inputs to your computer. This traffic stays between your phone and your computer; it does not go through our servers. The App may display the name of the connected computer.

Motion sensors: the gyroscope and accelerometer are used only for motion steering/aiming while a controller is open. Sensor data is processed on the device and is not stored or shared.

A foreground service notification may be shown to keep the controller connection active while you play.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process the device advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP, device and app information, and ad interaction data.

Where required (for example in the EEA, UK, Switzerland and certain US states), the App asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices (shown when applicable). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

Ads are never shown while a controller is in use.

Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

4. In-app review

If you choose to rate the App, Google Play's in-app review is used. The rating is handled by Google Play; we do not receive your identity.

5. Data sharing

We do not sell personal data. Data is processed by the service provider listed above (Google) only to provide advertising and reviews.

6. Data retention

Local data stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App. Data collected by Google is retained according to Google's policies.

7. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

8. Security

Data in transit to Google services is encrypted (HTTPS). Controller connections stay on your local network, USB cable or Bluetooth link.

9. Your rights

Depending on your location you may have rights to access, correct or delete data. For data held by Google, use Google's tools. For any question, contact contact@atdigitalstudio.com.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date shows the latest version.
