Privacy Policy — File Transfer & Share: Send Files

Last updated: November 19, 2026 · Developer: AT Digital Studio · Contact: contact@atdigitalstudio.com

This policy explains how the Android app File Transfer & Share: Send Files (com.filetransfer.share, "the App") handles information.

1. Direct transfer: no server involved

When you send or receive files with Direct Transfer, the App starts a temporary web server on your phone and the other device connects to it over your local Wi-Fi network or hotspot (by scanning a QR code or opening a local address in a browser). Files go straight from one device to the other. They are never uploaded to our servers or to any cloud, and we have no access to them.

Each session uses a random, single-use address and is closed when the transfer ends, is cancelled or times out. Received files are saved in the folder you choose (by default Download/FileTransfer on your device).

2. Share by Link: temporary storage

Share by Link is optional. When you use it, the files you select are uploaded over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) to temporary storage on Cloudflare R2, operated for us by Cloudflare. Anyone who has the link can download the files in a browser.

We store only the files themselves and the information needed to serve them: file names, sizes, a title, and the expiry date. No account is needed and we do not ask for your name, e-mail or phone number.

Files are deleted automatically when the link expires (after 1, 3 or 7 days, as you choose), by an automatic cleanup that runs every hour. You can also delete a link at any time in the App (Share Links). Do not share sensitive files with people you do not trust: whoever has the link can download them until it expires.

As with any website, Cloudflare processes technical data such as IP addresses to deliver and protect the service: Cloudflare privacy policy.

3. History stays on your device

The transfer history (file names, sizes, dates, device type, links you created) is stored only in the App's private storage on your device. It is never sent to us. You can clear it at any time in Settings, and uninstalling the App deletes it. Device backup is disabled for the App.

4. Permissions

The App uses network access (local transfers and Share by Link), a foreground service and a notification while a transfer is active (so it keeps working with the screen off), and the Android file pickers to let you choose the files or the folder you want. It does not access your camera, microphone, contacts or location, and only reads the files you pick.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and shows ads through Google AdMob (banner, native, interstitial, app open and optional rewarded ads). To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process the device advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP, device and app information, and ad interaction data. Ads never receive your files or their content.

Rewarded ads are always optional: watching one unlocks the 7-day link duration. If no video is available, the option stays free.

Where required (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, under the GDPR, and in certain US states), the App asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) before any ad is requested. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

6. Google Play features

The App uses Google Play In-App Review (to let you rate the App) and Google Play In-App Updates (to download new versions). These features are provided by Google Play and are subject to Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

7. No account, no analytics

The App does not require an account. It does not use third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.

8. Data sharing

We do not sell personal data and we do not share it for marketing. The only data processed by third parties is: the files you choose to upload with Share by Link (Cloudflare, section 2), and the advertising and Google Play technical data processed by Google (sections 5 and 6).

9. Data retention

Direct transfers are not stored by us at all. Share by Link files are kept only until the link expires (at most 7 days) or until you delete the link. The history stays on your device until you clear it or uninstall the App. Data collected by Google is retained according to Google's policies.

10. Security

Direct-transfer sessions use unguessable random addresses, accept a single receiving device and can require your confirmation before any file is received. Share by Link traffic is encrypted with HTTPS and links are random and hard to guess.

11. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

12. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR), you may have rights to access, correct or delete data. You can delete your share links in the App at any time, and your history is fully under your control on your device. For data held by Google, use Google's tools. For any question, contact contact@atdigitalstudio.com.

13. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date shows the latest version. See also our Terms of Use.
