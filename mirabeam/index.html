Privacy Policy — MiraBeam: Screen Mirroring & TV Cast

Last updated: November 2, 2026 · Developer: AT Digital Studio · Contact: contact@atdigitalstudio.com

This policy explains how the Android app MiraBeam: Screen Mirroring & TV Cast (com.mirabeam.screencast, "the App") handles information.

In short: your screen, photos, videos, music and documents go directly from your phone to the TV you choose on your local Wi-Fi network. They are never uploaded to us or stored by us. The App does not require an account.

1. Screen mirroring

When you start screen mirroring, Android asks for your permission before the App can capture your screen. While mirroring is active, Android shows a notification and everything visible on your screen, including notifications, is captured. The captured image is encoded on your phone and sent only to the TV or web browser you selected on your local network. It is not recorded, not saved and not sent to us. Sound stays on the phone. You can stop mirroring at any time from the App or the notification.

When you use "Mirror to a TV web browser", the App shows a local address (for example http://192.168.x.x:port/…) that contains a random code. Any device on the same Wi-Fi network that opens this exact address can view the stream until you stop mirroring. Use this mode only on networks you trust.

2. Photos, videos, music and documents

To show your photos, videos and music, the App asks for access to your media (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, READ_MEDIA_AUDIO, or storage access on older Android versions). PDF documents are opened only when you pick them with the Android file picker. The files you cast are served by a small temporary server that runs inside the App on your phone, reachable only on your local network, so the TV can play them. Files are not copied, uploaded or sent to us.

3. Local network and TV discovery

To find TVs and streaming devices, the App searches your local network using standard protocols (SSDP/UPnP, DLNA, mDNS, Google Cast, Roku). This reveals the names, models and local IP addresses of devices on your network to the App. The App also reads your Wi-Fi connection state and, when Android provides it, the network name, to show the connection status. This information stays on your device.

When you cast to a Chromecast or Google TV, the Google Cast framework provided by Google is used. Google may process technical data needed to operate Cast according to its privacy policy.

4. Data stored on your device

Settings, favorite and recent devices (name, nickname, brand, local IP address) and your casting history (device, type and duration of each session) are stored locally on your device. They are not sent to us and are excluded from Android cloud backup. You can clear the history in History → Clear, remove favorites in the App, or delete everything by clearing the App's data or uninstalling it.

5. Web browser

The App includes a web browser to find compatible media online. Websites you visit receive the usual information a browser sends (such as your IP address) and may use cookies, according to their own privacy policies. We do not receive your browsing history.

6. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads through Google AdMob, including banners, full-screen ads, app open ads and optional rewarded videos (watched only if you choose to, in exchange for a period without ads). To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process the device advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP, device and app information, and ad interaction data.

Where required (for example in the EEA, UK, Switzerland and certain US states), the App asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices (shown when applicable). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. Ads are paused while a cast or mirroring session is active.

Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

7. Analytics, crash reports and configuration (Firebase)

The App may use Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics to understand how features are used and to fix crashes, and Firebase Remote Config to adjust settings such as ad frequency without an update. Collected data may include app interactions, device model, OS version, app version, crash traces and an app-instance identifier. This data is not used to identify you personally. You can turn analytics and crash reports off in Settings → Share anonymous usage data.

Firebase privacy information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

8. Purchases and reviews

If in-app purchases are offered, payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We never receive your payment details; we only receive confirmation of the purchase so the App can unlock the feature. When you rate the App, the review is handled by Google Play under your Google account.

9. Contacting us

If you send feedback from the App, your e-mail app opens with a pre-filled message. We receive only what you choose to send: your e-mail address, your message and, if you leave the option checked, the App version and Android version. We use it only to answer you.

10. Permissions

  • Internet, network and Wi-Fi state, multicast: discover TVs and stream on your local network; load ads.
  • Photos, videos and audio: list and cast your media.
  • Notifications and foreground services: show the ongoing casting or mirroring notification.
  • Screen capture (asked by Android each time): screen mirroring.
  • Advertising ID: ads, as described in section 6.

11. Data sharing

We do not sell personal data. Data is processed by the service providers listed above (Google) only to provide advertising, analytics, crash reporting, casting and purchases.

12. Data retention

Local data stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App. Data collected by Google is retained according to Google's policies. Feedback e-mails are kept only as long as needed to handle your request.

13. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

14. Security

Data in transit to Google services is encrypted (HTTPS). Streaming to your TV stays on your local network, which is not encrypted by the TV protocols; use the App on networks you trust.

15. Your rights

Depending on your location you may have rights to access, correct or delete data. For data held by Google, use Google's tools. For any question, contact contact@atdigitalstudio.com.

16. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date shows the latest version.
