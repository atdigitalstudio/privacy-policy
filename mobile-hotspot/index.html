Privacy Policy — Mobile Hotspot: Portable WiFi

Last updated: November 23, 2026 · Developer: AT Digital Studio · Contact: contact@atdigitalstudio.com

This policy explains how the Android app Mobile Hotspot: Portable WiFi (com.wifi.hotspotrepeater, "the App") handles information.

In short: the App helps you open your phone's hotspot settings, create a local network and share it by QR code. Your settings stay on your device. The App does not require an account, and we do not run any server or database about you. The App does not provide a data plan or internet access of its own.

1. Location and nearby devices permissions

To create the local network (Android "local-only hotspot"), Android requires the location permission, and on Android 13 and later also the "Nearby Wi-Fi devices" permission (declared as not used for location). The App asks for these permissions only for this purpose. The App never reads your GPS position, and no location data is stored, sent to us or shared by the App.

2. Hotspot, QR code and connected devices

  • Local network and QR code: the network name and password generated by Android are shown on screen and encoded in a QR code drawn on your device. They are never uploaded.
  • Connected devices: the App looks for devices connected to your phone's hotspot (local IP addresses) directly on your phone. The list is shown on screen only and is never uploaded.
  • Traffic chart: the App reads your phone's total network traffic counters from Android to draw the live chart. These numbers stay on your device.

3. Data stored on your device

Your preferences (Smart Auto-Turn Off rules, advanced limits, language) and small counters used by the App (number of launches, review prompt timing, ad frequency limits) are stored locally on your device. They are not sent to us. They may be included in your personal Android backup (Google account) if you have enabled device backup. Uninstalling the App deletes all local data.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads through Google AdMob: banners, native ads (always labelled "Ad"), full-screen interstitial ads, app open ads and optional rewarded videos (watching one unlocks advanced limits for 24 hours). To serve and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process the device advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, device and app information, ad interaction data and diagnostic data.

Where required (for example in the EEA, UK, Switzerland and certain US states), the App asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before any ad is requested. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options (shown when applicable). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

5. Reviews and updates

The App may occasionally show Google Play's In-App Review dialog; your rating and review are handled by Google under your Google account. The App uses Google Play's In-App Updates to tell you when a new version is available.

6. Contacting us

If you write to us, we receive only what you choose to send (your e-mail address and message) and use it only to answer you.

7. Permissions

  • Location (precise): required by Android to create the local network, as described in section 1.
  • Nearby Wi-Fi devices, Android 13+: create the local network, not used for location.
  • Wi-Fi state and change Wi-Fi state: start and stop the local network and read its state.
  • Internet and network state: ads, consent, in-app updates and reviews.
  • Foreground service (connected device) and notifications: keep Smart Auto-Turn Off running while it watches your local network, with a simple status notification.
  • Advertising ID: ads, as described in section 4.

8. Data sharing

We do not sell personal data. We do not collect personal data on our own servers. Data is processed only by Google (AdMob, User Messaging Platform and Google Play services) to provide advertising, consent management, reviews and updates.

9. Data retention

Local data stays on your device until you uninstall the App. Data collected by Google is retained according to Google's policies. E-mails are kept only as long as needed to handle your request.

10. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

11. Security

The App's internet traffic (ads, consent, updates) uses encrypted connections (HTTPS). The local network uses the WPA security generated by Android.

12. Your rights

Depending on your location you may have rights to access, correct or delete data. For data held by Google, use Google's tools. For any question, contact contact@atdigitalstudio.com.

13. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date shows the latest version.
