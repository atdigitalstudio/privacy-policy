Privacy Policy — CV Studio: Resume Maker & AI

Last updated: November 18, 2026 · Developer: AT Digital Studio · Contact: contact@atdigitalstudio.com

This policy explains how the Android app CV Studio: Resume Maker & AI (com.cvstudio.resumemaker, "the App") handles information.

1. Your CVs stay on your device

Your CVs, cover letters, profile photo and settings are stored in the App's private storage on your device. We do not run any server that stores your documents, and we have no access to them.

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include the App's data (CVs, letters, photo and settings) in your encrypted device backup, so that it can be restored on a new phone. This backup is managed by Google and your Android settings, not by us.

When you share, print or save a PDF, it goes only to the app or location you choose. You can delete any document in the App at any time. Uninstalling the App removes all of its data from the device.

2. AI writing assistant (optional)

The AI features (summary, bullet improvement, grammar, translation, keywords, cover letter) are optional and only run when you tap them, after you accept an in-app consent notice.

When you use them, the text you choose to submit (for example a section of your CV and, if you add it, a job description) is sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) to our AI service hosted on Cloudflare Workers, which generates the suggestion with an AI model (Cloudflare Workers AI or Anthropic). The text is used only to produce the suggestion. We do not store it, we do not use it to train models and we do not use it for advertising.

To limit abuse, each request includes a random installation identifier generated by the App. It is not linked to your name, e-mail, phone or advertising ID, and is only used to count the number of AI requests per day (the counter expires after about one day).

Do not include information you do not want to send (such as identity numbers) in text you submit to the AI. You always review a suggestion before it is applied to your CV.

Providers' policies: Cloudflare · Anthropic

3. Importing a CV (on device)

When you import a PDF or a photo, you pick the file with the Android system picker, and the App only receives the file you select. The text is extracted on your device. Scanned documents and photos are read with Google ML Kit text recognition, which runs on your device through Google Play services; your files are not sent to us. Google Play services may send Google technical information about the performance and use of this feature: https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms

The App does not use the camera, microphone, contacts or location, and does not request access to your photo library or storage.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and shows ads through Google AdMob (banner, interstitial, app open and optional rewarded ads). To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process the device advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP, device and app information, and ad interaction data. Ads never receive the content of your CVs or letters.

Rewarded ads are always optional: you choose to watch one to unlock a template or a color, or to get extra AI requests for the day.

Where required (for example in the EEA, UK, Switzerland and certain US states), the App asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & ad choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

5. Google Play features

The App uses Google Play In-App Review (to let you rate the App; you choose your own rating and whether to leave a comment) and Google Play In-App Updates (to download new versions). These features are provided by Google Play and are subject to Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

6. No account, no analytics

The App does not require an account and does not ask for an e-mail or password. Personal details you type into your CV (name, phone, e-mail, address, photo) stay in your document on your device. The App does not use third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.

7. Data sharing

We do not sell personal data. The only data processed by third parties is: the text you explicitly submit to the AI assistant (section 2), and the advertising and Google Play / ML Kit technical data processed by Google (sections 3 to 5).

8. Data retention

Documents stay on your device until you delete them or uninstall the App. AI text is processed only for the time needed to return the suggestion. The daily AI counter expires after about one day. Data collected by Google is retained according to Google's policies.

9. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

10. Security

Documents are kept in the App's private storage, which other apps cannot access. All network traffic (AI service, Google services) is encrypted with HTTPS.

11. Your rights

Depending on your location you may have rights to access, correct or delete data. Your CVs are fully under your control on your device. For data held by Google, use Google's tools. For any question, contact contact@atdigitalstudio.com.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date shows the latest version. See also our Terms of Use.
