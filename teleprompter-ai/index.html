Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Amine Tahtoub / ATDIGITALSTUDIO ("we") publishes the Teleprompter AI: Video Script app (Android package com.teleprompter.videoscript.pro, "the app"). This policy explains what the app accesses and why. The app needs no account and we never sell personal data.

Stays on your phoneScripts, recorded videos, your name, settings and language. Camera and microphone are used only while you record.
Sent only when neededThe AI generator sends the topic you type to our server. Ads (AdMob), consent (UMP), app updates and reviews (Google Play).

Data that stays on your device

  • Scripts (title, text, favorites) are stored in the app's local database on your phone. Text files you import are read on your device only.
  • Videos are recorded to the app's private storage on your phone and are never uploaded to us. They are copied to your gallery or shared with another app only when you tap Save to gallery or Share.
  • Camera and microphone are used only while the recording screen is open, to film you and capture your voice. You can record without the microphone. Nothing is streamed or sent anywhere.
  • Your name (optional, shown in the greeting), settings (theme, text size, scroll speed, mirror mode, video quality), language and the daily count of AI generations are stored on your device only.
  • Android backup: if you have enabled backup in your phone settings, Android may include your scripts and settings in your own Google backup so they can be restored on a new phone. Videos are not included.

Data shared with third parties

  • AI script generator teleprompter-ai.sendfast.workers.dev
    When you tap Generate Script, the app sends our server, hosted on Cloudflare Workers, the topic you type, the content type, the tone, the duration and the app language. They are used only to write the script with Cloudflare Workers AI and to send it back to you. We do not store the topic or the script and do not link them to you. Please do not type sensitive personal information in the topic. Cloudflare processes technical data such as your IP address to deliver and protect the service; see the Cloudflare privacy policy.
  • Advertising Google AdMob
    The app shows ads with Google AdMob (banner, interstitial, app open and optional rewarded video ads for extra AI generations). Google may collect the advertising ID, IP address, device information and ad interactions to serve and measure ads, and to personalize them if you agree. Ads are never shown while you record. See how Google uses data in ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.
  • Consent message Google UMP
    Where required (EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regions), a privacy message from Google's User Messaging Platform is shown at first launch. To know whether it must be shown and to store your choice, it receives your IP address and device information. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy choices. See policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • App updates and reviews Google Play
    The app uses Google Play In-App Updates to tell you when a new version is available and to download it if you choose, and Google Play In-App Review to let you rate the app without leaving it. Both are provided by Google Play, which receives your Google Play account and device information under its own privacy policy. We never see your Google account or who wrote a review.
  • Sharing
    When you share a video, Android's share menu hands it to the app you choose, which applies its own privacy policy.

The app has no analytics and no crash reporting service. All network traffic is encrypted (HTTPS).

Retention and your choices

Everything stored on your device stays there until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app. Because we keep no personal data on our servers, there is nothing for us to delete; for data processed by Google, use the links above.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.
