Privacy Policy — Teleprompter AI: Video Script

Last updated: November 23, 2026 · Developer: AT Digital Studio · Contact: contact@atdigitalstudio.com

This policy explains how the Android app Teleprompter AI: Video Script (com.teleprompter.videoscript.pro, "the App") handles information.

In short: the App lets you write scripts, read them on a scrolling teleprompter and record videos with your phone's camera while the script scrolls. Your scripts and videos stay on your phone. The App has no account and does not upload your scripts or videos. The only data sent off your device is the topic you type in the optional AI script generator, and the data processed by Google to show ads and run Google Play features, as described below.

1. Scripts, camera and videos

Scripts (title, text, favorites) are stored only in the App's local database on your phone. When you import a text file, it is read on your device only.

The camera and microphone are used only while the recording screen is open. Videos (MP4) are recorded to the App's private storage on your phone and are never uploaded by the App. A video is copied to your gallery (Movies) only when you press "Save to gallery". When you choose "Share", Android's share sheet sends the video to the app you pick; we do not see it. You can record without the microphone; the video then has no sound.

2. AI script generator

When you press "Generate Script", the App sends our server, hosted on Cloudflare Workers (teleprompter-ai.sendfast.workers.dev), the topic you type, the content type (TikTok, Reels, YouTube, presentation, podcast), the tone, the duration and the App language. This information is used only to write the script with Cloudflare Workers AI and send it back to you. We do not store the topic or the generated script and do not link them to you. Please do not type sensitive personal information in the topic. Cloudflare processes technical data such as your IP address to deliver and protect the service: https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/.

AI-generated text may contain mistakes; review it before recording.

3. Data stored on your device

The App stores a few local settings: your optional name shown in the greeting, your chosen language, appearance (theme, accent color, text color, font, text size, line spacing, margins), teleprompter options (scroll speed, mirror mode, video quality), the number of free AI generations used today and any bonus generations unlocked, and a counter of successful recordings and exports used to ask for a rating at most once every 30 days, and when ads were last shown (to limit how often they appear). Your scripts and settings stay on your device and may be included in your Android device backup if backup is enabled; videos are not included. You can delete them by clearing the App's data or uninstalling it.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and shows ads through Google AdMob: banners, full-screen (interstitial) ads, app open ads and optional rewarded ads ("watch an ad to unlock extra AI generations for today"). Ads are never shown during a recording. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process the device advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP, device and app information, and ad interaction data.

Where required (for example in the EEA, UK, Switzerland and certain US states), the App asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy choices (shown when applicable). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

5. Google Play features

The App uses Google Play In-App Review to let you rate the App, and Google Play In-App Updates to tell you when a new version is available and install it. Both are handled by Google Play under your Google account; we do not receive your account details or see who wrote a review.

6. Permissions

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA): record videos while the teleprompter scrolls. Used only while the recording screen is open.
  • Microphone (android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO): optional, records your voice in the video; used only while recording.
  • Storage (WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, Android 9 and earlier only): save a video to your gallery. On Android 10 and later no storage permission is used.
  • Internet and network state (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): AI script generator, ads, consent form and Google Play features.
  • Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS): added by the Google Mobile Ads SDK for ads, as described in section 4.

The App does not request access to your location, contacts, call logs, or to photos and videos created by other apps.

7. Data sharing

We do not sell personal data. The topic you type in the AI generator is processed by Cloudflare only to write your script. Data is processed by Google only to provide advertising, consent management, in-app reviews and in-app updates. We do not use any other analytics, crash reporting or tracking service.

8. Data retention

Scripts, videos and settings stay on your device until you delete them or uninstall the App (videos saved to your gallery remain there after uninstalling unless you delete them). AI requests are not stored by us. Data collected by Google is retained according to Google's policies. E-mails you send us are kept only as long as needed to answer you.

9. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

10. Security

Communication with our AI server and with Google services is encrypted (HTTPS). The App does not send your scripts or videos to any server.

11. Your rights

Depending on your location you may have rights to access, correct or delete data. Because we keep no personal data on our servers, deleting your scripts and videos in the App, or uninstalling it, erases your data. For data held by Google, use Google's tools. For any question, contact contact@atdigitalstudio.com.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date shows the latest version.
