Privacy Policy — USB Connector: Phone to TV

Last updated: November 9, 2026 · Developer: AT Digital Studio · Contact: contact@atdigitalstudio.com

This policy explains how the Android app USB Connector: Phone to TV (com.usbtvhdmi.connector, "the App") handles information.

In short: the App guides you to connect your phone to a TV with a USB-C to HDMI adapter and opens your phone's screen mirroring settings. It does not record your screen, does not access your photos, files, contacts or location, and does not require an account. The only data collected off your device is the data processed by Google to show ads and run Google Play features, as described below.

1. Phone compatibility check

When you tap "Check Phone", the App reads technical information that Android exposes on your device: whether USB host and USB accessory modes are supported, the number of USB devices currently attached, whether an external display is connected, Wi-Fi Direct support, and your device manufacturer, model and Android version. This information is shown to you on screen only. It is not stored, not uploaded and not sent to us.

2. Screen mirroring

The "Screen Mirror" feature only opens your phone's own Cast / Smart View / screen mirroring settings. Mirroring is then handled entirely by Android and your phone manufacturer. The App does not capture, record or transmit your screen.

3. Data stored on your device

The App stores a few local preferences: whether you completed the introduction, your chosen app language, the number of times the App was opened, the last App version used (to show "What's new" after an update) and whether you already chose to rate the App. These preferences stay on your device and may be included in your Android device backup if backup is enabled in your system settings. You can delete them by clearing the App's data or uninstalling it.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and shows ads through Google AdMob: banners, native ads, full-screen (interstitial) ads and app open ads. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process the device advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP, device and app information, and ad interaction data.

Where required (for example in the EEA, UK, Switzerland and certain US states), the App asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from the menu (⋮) → Privacy settings (shown when applicable). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

5. Google Play features

The App uses Google Play In-App Review to let you rate and comment on the App, and Google Play In-App Updates to tell you when a new version is available and install it. Reviews and updates are handled by Google Play under your Google account; we do not receive your account details. We only see public reviews on Google Play, like anyone else.

6. Sharing the App and opening links

If you choose "Share app", Android's share sheet opens with a link to the App; we do not see who you share it with. Links such as this privacy policy open in your browser.

7. Permissions

  • Internet and network state: load ads, consent forms and Google Play features.
  • Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID): ads, as described in section 4.
  • USB host feature (optional): read USB capabilities for the compatibility check. The App does not read or write data on connected USB devices.

The App does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or files.

8. Data sharing

We do not sell personal data. Data is processed by Google only to provide advertising, consent management, in-app reviews and in-app updates. We do not use any other analytics or tracking service.

9. Data retention

Local preferences stay on your device until you delete them or uninstall the App. Data collected by Google is retained according to Google's policies. E-mails you send us are kept only as long as needed to answer you.

10. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

11. Security

Communication with Google services is encrypted (HTTPS). The App itself does not send your personal information to our servers.

12. Your rights

Depending on your location you may have rights to access, correct or delete data. For data held by Google, use Google's tools. For any question, contact contact@atdigitalstudio.com.

13. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date shows the latest version.
