Privacy Policy — Sudoku Pro: Daily Puzzle

Last updated: October 24, 2026 · Developer: AT Digital Studio · Contact: contact@atdigitalstudio.com

This policy explains how the Android app Sudoku Pro: Daily Puzzle (com.sudokupro.dailypuzzle, "the App") handles information.

1. Data stored on your device

Game progress, statistics, achievements, daily challenge history and settings are stored locally on your device. They are not sent to us. Android may include them in your device backup (Google account backup) according to your device settings. You can delete them with Settings → Reset statistics and progress, or by uninstalling the App.

The App does not require an account and does not ask for your name, e-mail, contacts, location, photos or files.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process the device advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP, device and app information, and ad interaction data.

Where required (for example in the EEA, UK, Switzerland and certain US states), the App asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices (shown when applicable). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

3. Analytics and crash reports (Firebase)

The App may use Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics to understand usage (for example which difficulty levels are played) and to fix crashes. Collected data may include app interactions, device model, OS version, app version, crash traces and an app-instance identifier. This data is not used to identify you personally. You can turn it off in Settings → Share usage analytics.

Firebase privacy information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

4. Data sharing

We do not sell personal data. Data is processed by the service providers listed above (Google) only to provide advertising, analytics and crash reporting.

5. Data retention

Local data stays on your device until you reset it or uninstall the App. Data collected by Google is retained according to Google's policies.

6. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

7. Security

Data in transit to Google services is encrypted (HTTPS).

8. Your rights

Depending on your location you may have rights to access, correct or delete data. For data held by Google, use Google's tools. For any question, contact contact@atdigitalstudio.com.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date shows the latest version.
