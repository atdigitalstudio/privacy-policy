Privacy Policy — Moto Diagnostic Pro

Last updated: November 3, 2026 · Developer: AT Digital Studio · Contact: contact@atdigitalstudio.com

This policy explains how the Android app Moto Diagnostic Pro (com.motodiagnosticpro, "the App") handles information.

In short: everything you enter (motorcycles, maintenance, customers, quotes, invoices, diagnostic sessions) is stored only on your phone. The App has no account and no server of ours; we never receive your records. The App is free and supported by advertising (Google AdMob).

1. Diagnostics with an OBD adapter

To read fault codes and live values, the App connects to an ELM327-compatible adapter over Bluetooth Classic, Bluetooth Low Energy or USB. Android asks for the Bluetooth permissions (BLUETOOTH_CONNECT, BLUETOOTH_SCAN) before the App can find and connect to adapters. Bluetooth scanning is declared as not used to derive your location; on Android 11 and older, Android requires the location permission for any Bluetooth scan, and the App uses it only for that. Data read from the motorcycle (fault codes, VIN, protocol, live values such as engine speed or temperature) stays on your device and is saved only when you choose to save a session.

2. Data stored on your device

The App stores locally: your motorcycles (brand, model, year, VIN, mileage, notes), service records and reminders, diagnostic sessions, repair orders, saved troubleshooting cases, checklists, notes, and your settings. If you use the workshop features, it also stores the customers you add (name, phone, e-mail, address, company ID) and your quotes and invoices, together with your business details and logo. This data is not sent to us and is excluded from Android cloud backup.

If you record information about your own customers, you are responsible for having a valid reason to do so and for informing them, as required by the data protection law that applies to you.

You can delete everything at any time in Settings → Delete all my records, or by clearing the App's data or uninstalling it.

3. Backups, PDF and sharing

"Export backup" writes a file to a location you choose with the Android file picker. PDF reports, quotes and invoices are generated on your phone and are shared only when you pick a destination in the Android share sheet (for example e-mail, WhatsApp, printing or cloud storage). From then on, the app you choose handles the file under its own policy. Your logo is selected with the Android photo picker; only the image you pick is copied into the App.

4. Maintenance reminders

If you turn on reminders, the App checks your service schedules on the device and shows local notifications. On Android 13 and newer, Android asks for the notification permission first. No data is sent to a server for this.

5. Usage statistics

An optional setting, off by default, counts which modules you open. These counters stay on your device and are never sent to us. VINs, customers and notes are never included. The App does not use Firebase or any other analytics service.

6. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads through Google AdMob: banners, native ads in lists, full-screen ads between tasks, app open ads and optional rewarded videos (watched only if you choose to, to unlock extra library content for a limited time). To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process the device advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP, device and app information, and ad interaction data.

Where required (for example in the EEA, UK, Switzerland and certain US states), the App asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices (shown when applicable). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. Full-screen ads are not shown while a diagnostic connection is active.

Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

7. Reviews

When you rate the App, the review is handled by Google Play under your Google account.

8. Contacting us

If you contact support from the App, your e-mail app opens. We receive only what you choose to send and use it only to answer you.

9. Permissions

  • Internet and network state: load ads.
  • Bluetooth (and location on Android 11 and older, for Bluetooth scanning only): find and connect to your OBD adapter.
  • USB (asked by Android when you plug in an adapter): connect to a USB adapter.
  • Notifications: maintenance reminders.
  • Advertising ID: ads, as described in section 6.

10. Data sharing

We do not sell personal data and we do not receive your records. Data is processed by Google only to provide advertising and Google Play services.

11. Data retention

Local data stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App. Data collected by Google is retained according to Google's policies. Support e-mails are kept only as long as needed to handle your request.

12. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

13. Security

Your records are stored in the App's private storage on your phone. Communication with Google services is encrypted (HTTPS). Bluetooth and USB links to the adapter are local to your phone.

14. Your rights

Depending on your location you may have rights to access, correct or delete data. Your records are on your device and under your control. For data held by Google, use Google's tools. For any question, contact contact@atdigitalstudio.com.

15. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date shows the latest version.
