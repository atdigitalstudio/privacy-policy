Privacy Policy — Weather Alarm: Forecast & Radar

Last updated: November 11, 2026 · Developer: AT Digital Studio · Contact: contact@atdigitalstudio.com

This policy explains how the Android app Weather Alarm: Forecast & Radar (com.atdigitalstudio.weatheralarm, "the App") handles information.

In short: the App needs a location (a city you pick, or your device location if you allow it) to show the weather. Your alarms, saved cities and settings stay on your device. The App does not require an account and we do not run any database about you.

1. Location

You can either search for a city or let the App use your device location. Location is requested only when you tap "Use my location" (or open the outdoor tools) and is used only in the foreground to show the weather where you are; it is never collected in the background. Your device may turn the coordinates into a place name using the Android geocoding service (provided by Google on most devices).

To get a forecast, the App sends the coordinates of the selected place (rounded to 4 decimals, about 10 m) and the place name to our weather server (see section 2). Coordinates are not linked to any identifier of you or your device.

2. Weather data and our server

Weather requests go through our own server, hosted on Cloudflare Workers, which forwards them to the weather provider and briefly caches the results (up to 30 minutes for forecasts, 24 hours for city searches) to keep the App fast. We do not store requests in any database and we do not build profiles. As with any internet service, Cloudflare processes your IP address to deliver the request.

  • Forecasts: MET Norway (Norwegian Meteorological Institute, api.met.no), licensed CC BY 4.0. MET Norway receives only the place coordinates from our server, not your IP address.
  • City search: the text you type is sent to Photon (komoot), based on OpenStreetMap data (© OpenStreetMap contributors).
  • Official weather alerts (when available): WeatherAPI.com may receive the place coordinates from our server.
  • Precipitation map: map and satellite precipitation images (NASA GPM IMERG) are downloaded by your device directly from NASA GIBS (gibs.earthdata.nasa.gov), which therefore sees your IP address, like any website.

3. Alarms, cities and settings stored on your device

Your alarms (time, days, weather conditions, ringtone choice), saved cities, the last weather downloaded, destinations you save in the outdoor tools and your preferences (units, theme, language) are stored locally on your device. They are not sent to us. They may be included in your personal Android backup (Google account) if you have enabled device backup. Clearing the App's data or uninstalling it deletes them.

4. Outdoor tools (compass, GPS, navigation)

The compass uses your device's motion sensors; the GPS screen shows your position, accuracy and speed. All of this is computed on your device. Coordinates leave the App only if you choose "Copy", "Share", "Open in Maps" or "Route", in which case they go to the app you choose.

5. Notifications and alarms

The App uses notifications for ringing alarms and weather notices, and Android's exact-alarm and full-screen features so alarms ring on time, even on the lock screen. To prepare the weather briefing, the App may refresh the forecast for the alarm's place shortly before an alarm rings.

6. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and shows ads through Google AdMob: banners, native ads (always labelled "Ad"), full-screen ads and app open ads. Full-screen ads are never shown while an alarm is ringing. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process the device advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP, device and app information, and ad interaction data.

Where required (for example in the EEA, UK, Switzerland and certain US states), the App asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy settings (shown when applicable). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

7. Reviews

When you rate the App, Google Play's In-App Review is used and the review is handled under your Google account.

8. Contacting us

If you write to us, we receive only what you choose to send (your e-mail address and message) and use it only to answer you.

9. Permissions

  • Location (approximate and precise): weather where you are and the outdoor tools, only while you use them.
  • Internet and network state: download weather, maps and ads; show the offline state.
  • Notifications (Android 13+): ringing alarms and weather notices.
  • Exact alarms, full-screen intent, wake lock, run at startup: ring alarms on time, on the lock screen, and restore them after a restart.
  • Foreground service (media playback): play the alarm sound reliably while it rings.
  • Vibration: vibrate when an alarm rings (can be turned off per alarm).
  • Advertising ID: ads, as described in section 6.

10. Data sharing

We do not sell personal data. We do not collect personal data on our own servers. Data is processed by the services listed above only to provide weather data, maps, city search and advertising.

11. Data retention

Local data stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App. Our server only keeps short-lived caches of weather responses (at most 24 hours) that are not linked to you. Data collected by Google is retained according to Google's policies. E-mails are kept only as long as needed to handle your request.

12. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

13. Security

All network traffic of the App uses encrypted connections (HTTPS); unencrypted traffic is disabled.

14. Weather information disclaimer

Forecasts, satellite precipitation estimates and alerts come from third parties and may be incomplete, delayed or wrong. Do not rely on the App alone for safety decisions; follow your national weather service and local authorities.

15. Your rights

Depending on your location you may have rights to access, correct or delete data. For data held by Google, use Google's tools. For any question, contact contact@atdigitalstudio.com.

16. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date shows the latest version.
