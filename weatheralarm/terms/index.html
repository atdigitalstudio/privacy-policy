Weather data comes from third parties (MET Norway, NASA GIBS / GPM IMERG and, when available, official alert providers). Forecasts can be wrong, incomplete or delayed, and the precipitation map is a satellite estimate published with a delay of several hours, not live radar. Do not use the App as your only source for safety decisions; always follow your national weather service and local authorities.

3. Alarms

Alarms depend on your device: battery optimisation, "do not disturb", volume, permissions or a powered-off phone can delay or prevent an alarm. Keep notifications and exact alarms allowed for the App and check your volume. Do not rely on the App for critical wake-ups (medication, travel, work safety).

4. Outdoor tools
