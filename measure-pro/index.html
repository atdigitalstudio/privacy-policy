Privacy Policy — Measure Pro: Level & Ruler

Last updated: November 17, 2026 · Developer: AT Digital Studio · Contact: contact@atdigitalstudio.com

This policy explains how the Android app Measure Pro: Level & Ruler (com.measurepro.levelruler, "the App") handles information.

1. Your measurements stay on your device

Saved measurements (name, value, unit, tool and date), calibration values and settings are stored only in the App's private storage on your device. They are never uploaded to us or to any server, and we have no access to them.

If Android backup is enabled on your device, this data may be included in your Google account backup so it can be restored on a new device. This backup is managed by Google and is not accessible to us. You can turn it off in Android settings.

When you share or copy a measurement, it goes only to the app you choose (for example a messaging app or e-mail). You can delete any measurement from History at any time. Uninstalling the App removes all of its data from your device.

2. Camera

The camera is requested only when you open a camera-based tool (for example AR measure or the camera level). The live image is used on your device to display the view and compute measurements. The App does not take photos, record video or audio, and does not save or send camera images.

The App does not request access to your photos, files, contacts or location.

3. Sensors and augmented reality (Google ARCore)

The level, inclinometer, protractor and compass tools use your device's motion and magnetic sensors. This data is processed in real time on your device and is not stored or sent.

AR measurement uses Google Play Services for AR (ARCore), provided by Google. ARCore processes camera images on your device and may send Google technical information about the use of the service, as described by Google: https://developers.google.com/ar/develop/privacy-requirements

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process the device advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP, device and app information, and ad interaction data. Ads never receive your measurements.

Where required (for example in the EEA, UK, Switzerland and certain US states), the App asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy and ad choices (shown when applicable). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

5. Google Play features

The App uses Google Play In-App Review to let you rate the App. This feature is provided by Google Play and is subject to Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

6. No account, no analytics

The App does not require an account and does not ask for your name or e-mail. It does not use analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.

7. Data sharing

We do not sell personal data. We do not collect your measurements. The only data processed by a third party is the advertising data and the Google Play / ARCore technical data described above, processed by Google.

8. Data retention

Measurements stay on your device until you delete them or uninstall the App. Data collected by Google is retained according to Google's policies.

9. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

10. Security

Your data is kept in the App's private storage, which other apps cannot access. Data in transit to Google services is encrypted (HTTPS).

11. Accuracy disclaimer

Measurements depend on your device's sensors and camera and are provided for guidance only. They should not be relied on where precision is critical.

12. Your rights

Depending on your location you may have rights to access, correct or delete data. Your measurements are fully under your control on your device. For data held by Google, use Google's tools. For any question, contact contact@atdigitalstudio.com.

13. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date shows the latest version.
