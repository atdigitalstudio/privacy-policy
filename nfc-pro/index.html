Privacy Policy — NFC Pro: Tag Reader & Writer

Last updated: November 24, 2026 · Developer: AT Digital Studio · Contact: contact@atdigitalstudio.com

This policy explains how the Android app NFC Pro: Tag Reader & Writer (com.nfcpro.tagreaderwriter, "the App") handles information.

In short: the App reads, writes and analyzes NFC tags with your phone's NFC hardware. Everything you save stays on your device. The App does not require an account, and we do not run any server or database about you.

1. NFC tags

The App uses the NFC permission to read and write tags only while a reading, writing or analysis screen is open. The content of the tags you scan or write (links, text, contacts, Wi-Fi details, technical identifiers such as the tag UID) is processed on your device and is never sent to us or to any server by the App. Links found on a tag are opened only after you confirm, in the browser or app of your choice.

2. Data stored on your device

  • Saved tags: the scan results you choose to save (title, records, technical information).
  • History: a log of your read, write, erase, format and lock operations with their result.
  • Business card: the details you enter and, if you add one, a photo you pick from your gallery. The photo is shared only when you share the card yourself; it is never written to NFC tags.
  • Preferences: language, theme, the end time of an ad-free period, and small counters used for the review prompt timing.

This data is stored in the App's private storage (a local Room database and Android DataStore). It is not sent to us. It may be included in your personal Android backup (Google account) if you have enabled device backup. When you export your history or share a business card, the file goes only to the app you choose in the Android share sheet.

3. Deleting your data

You can delete saved tags, history and your business card (including its photo) at any time in Settings → Clear all data. Individual saved tags and history entries can also be deleted one by one. Uninstalling the App deletes all local data, including preferences.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and shows ads through Google AdMob: banners, native ads (always labelled "Ad"), full-screen interstitial ads, app open ads and optional rewarded videos (watching one removes all ads for 24 hours). Full-screen ads are never shown while a tag is being read or written. To serve and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process the device advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, device and app information, ad interaction data and diagnostic data. The content of your NFC tags is never shared with Google or with advertisers.

Where required (for example in the EEA, UK, Switzerland and certain US states), the App asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) before any ad is requested, in line with the GDPR. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy → Privacy options (shown when applicable). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

5. Reviews and updates

The App may occasionally show Google Play's In-App Review dialog; Google decides whether it appears, and your rating and review are handled by Google under your Google account. The App uses Google Play's In-App Updates to tell you when a new version is available and to download it through Google Play.

6. Contacting us

If you write to us, we receive only what you choose to send (your e-mail address and message) and use it only to answer you.

7. Permissions

  • NFC: read, write, erase, format and lock NFC tags, as described in section 1.
  • Internet and network state: ads, consent, in-app updates and reviews. The App never uploads your tags, history or business card.
  • Advertising ID: ads, as described in section 4.

The App does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files. Photos for the business card are chosen through the Android photo picker, which gives the App access only to the image you select.

8. Data sharing

We do not sell personal data. We do not collect personal data on our own servers. Data is processed only by Google (AdMob, User Messaging Platform and Google Play services) to provide advertising, consent management, reviews and updates.

9. Data retention

Local data stays on your device until you delete it in the App or uninstall the App. Data collected by Google is retained according to Google's policies. E-mails are kept only as long as needed to handle your request.

10. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

11. Security

Your data is kept in the App's private storage, which other apps cannot read. The App's internet traffic (ads, consent, updates) uses encrypted connections (HTTPS).

12. Your rights

Depending on your location you may have rights to access, correct or delete data. Data stored by the App is under your control on your device (section 3). For data held by Google, use Google's tools. For any question, contact contact@atdigitalstudio.com.

13. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date shows the latest version.
