Privacy Policy — OBD2 Scanner: Car Diagnostics

Last updated: November 20, 2026 · Developer: AT Digital Studio · Contact: contact@atdigitalstudio.com

This policy explains how the Android app OBD2 Scanner: Car Diagnostics (com.obd.carscanner, "the App") handles information.

In short: everything you enter (vehicles, diagnostic sessions, recordings, maintenance, parking spots, customers, quotes and invoices) is stored on your phone. The App has no account and no server of ours; we never receive your records. The free version is supported by advertising (Google AdMob); an optional Premium subscription removes ads.

1. Diagnostics with an OBD2 adapter

To read and clear fault codes and show live engine data, the App connects to an ELM327-compatible adapter over Bluetooth Classic, Bluetooth Low Energy or Wi-Fi. Android asks for the Bluetooth permissions (BLUETOOTH_CONNECT, BLUETOOTH_SCAN) before the App can find and connect to adapters. Bluetooth scanning is declared as not used to derive your location; on Android 11 and older, Android requires the location permission for any Bluetooth scan, and the App uses it only for that. Data read from the vehicle (fault codes, VIN, protocol, readiness monitors, freeze frame, live values such as engine speed or coolant temperature) stays on your device and is saved only when you choose to save a session or a recording.

2. Location: Quick Navigation, Parking Finder and guidance

Location (approximate or precise, as you allow) is used only while you use Quick Navigation, Parking Finder or in-app guidance, and only in the foreground:

  • To find places near you (fuel stations, garages, parking, etc.), your coordinates are sent to the public OpenStreetMap Overpass service, without any identifier of ours.
  • When you search an address, the text you type is sent to the OpenStreetMap Nominatim service.
  • When you start in-app guidance, your position and the destination are sent to an OSRM routing service (routing.openstreetmap.de or router.project-osrm.org) to calculate the route. Your position is followed on the device only while the guidance screen is open.
  • Map images are downloaded from OpenStreetMap tile servers for the area shown on screen.

These services receive your IP address as part of any internet request and handle it under their own policies (OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy). The App does not store your location history. Saved parking spots (position, optional note and photo) stay on your device.

3. Vehicle photos

To show a picture of your car on the Home screen, the App may search Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons for the make and model you entered (no other data is sent). The background of that picture is removed on your phone with Google ML Kit, which runs on the device. You can instead add your own photo with the camera or the Android photo picker; only the image you take or pick is copied into the App.

4. Data stored on your device

The App stores locally: your vehicles (make, model, year, VIN, mileage, photo), diagnostic sessions and fault codes, live data recordings, battery tests, service records and reminders, parking spots, your settings and, if you use the workshop features, the customers you add (name, phone, e-mail, address), your quotes and invoices, your business details and logo. This data is not sent to us.

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include the App's database and settings in your device backup (stored by Google, encrypted, and restored only to your own devices).

If you record information about your own customers, you are responsible for having a valid reason to do so and for informing them, as required by the data protection law that applies to you.

You can delete your data at any time by deleting items in the App, by clearing the App's data in Android settings, or by uninstalling it.

5. Exports, PDF and sharing

"Export backup", CSV exports, PDF diagnostic reports, quotes and invoices are generated on your phone and are shared only when you pick a destination in the Android share sheet or file picker (for example e-mail, WhatsApp, printing or cloud storage). From then on, the app you choose handles the file under its own policy.

6. Maintenance and parking reminders

If you turn on reminders, the App schedules local notifications on your device. On Android 13 and newer, Android asks for the notification permission first. No data is sent to a server for this.

7. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads through Google AdMob: banners, native ads, full-screen ads between tasks, app open ads and optional rewarded videos (watched only if you choose to, to unlock a Premium feature for a limited time). To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process the device advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP, device and app information, and ad interaction data.

Where required (for example in the EEA, UK, Switzerland and certain US states), the App asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices (shown when applicable). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. Full-screen ads are not shown while the App is connecting to an adapter or reading the vehicle.

Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

8. Purchases

The Premium subscription is processed by Google Play. The App does not receive your payment details; it only receives from Google Play whether your subscription is active.

9. Reviews and updates

The App may show Google Play's in-app review window and in-app update prompt. Your rating and review are handled by Google Play under your Google account; we do not see whether or how you rated. Updates are downloaded and installed by Google Play.

10. Permissions

  • Bluetooth (and location on Android 11 and older, for Bluetooth scanning only): find and connect to your OBD2 adapter.
  • Internet, network and Wi-Fi state: connect to Wi-Fi OBD2 adapters, load ads, maps, places and routes.
  • Location: Quick Navigation, Parking Finder and guidance, as described in section 2.
  • Notifications and run at startup: maintenance and parking reminders, restored after a reboot.
  • Advertising ID: ads, as described in section 7.

11. Data sharing

We do not sell personal data and we do not receive your records. Data leaves your device only as described above: to Google (advertising, Google Play services, optional Android backup) and to the OpenStreetMap, OSRM and Wikimedia (Wikipedia) services when you use the features that need them.

12. Data retention

Local data stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App. Data collected by Google and the other services above is retained according to their own policies. Support e-mails are kept only as long as needed to handle your request.

13. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

14. Security

Your records are stored in the App's private storage on your phone. Communication with Google, OpenStreetMap, OSRM and Wikimedia services is encrypted (HTTPS). Bluetooth and Wi-Fi links to the adapter are local.

15. Your rights

Depending on your location you may have rights to access, correct or delete data. Your records are on your device and under your control. For data held by Google, use Google's tools. For any question, contact contact@atdigitalstudio.com.

16. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date shows the latest version.
