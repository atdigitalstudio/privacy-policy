Privacy Policy — WiFi Analyzer & Network Tools

Last updated: November 18, 2026 · Developer: AT Digital Studio · Contact: contact@atdigitalstudio.com

This policy explains how the Android app WiFi Analyzer & Network Tools (com.tah.wifianalyzer, "the App") handles information.

In short: the App analyzes your WiFi and network directly on your device. Your test history and settings stay on your device. The App does not require an account and we do not run any database about you.

1. Location permission (WiFi name only)

Android only lets apps read the name (SSID) of the WiFi network you are connected to, and the list of nearby WiFi networks, when location access is granted. The App asks for location permission only for this purpose: to show your WiFi name, nearby networks and channels. The App never reads your GPS position, and location data is never stored, never sent to us and never shared. On Android 13 and later the App also uses the "Nearby WiFi devices" permission, declared as not used for location.

2. Network tools

  • Speed test: download and upload measurements are performed with Cloudflare's public speed test service (speed.cloudflare.com). Like any website, Cloudflare processes your IP address to deliver the test.
  • Ping, DNS lookup and port check: the App contacts only the host name or IP address you enter (by default one.one.one.one).
  • Network scanner: the App probes the devices of your local network directly from your phone to list them. The results are shown on screen only and are never uploaded.
  • WiFi check: combines the measurements above (signal, router response, DNS, latency, short download test) on your device.

3. Data stored on your device

Your history (speed test results, WiFi check scores, date, network name) and your preferences (theme, colors, units, language) are stored locally on your device. They are not sent to us. They may be included in your personal Android backup (Google account) if you have enabled device backup. You can clear the history in the App at any time; uninstalling the App deletes all local data.

4. Analytics and crash reports (Firebase)

The App uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand which features are used (for example "speed test completed" or "setting changed"), and Firebase Crashlytics to receive crash reports so we can fix bugs. These services may collect an app instance identifier, device model, Android version, app version, approximate country and usage events. They do not receive your WiFi name, your history or your location. See https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and shows ads through Google AdMob: banners, native ads (always labelled "Ad"), full-screen interstitial ads and app open ads. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process the device advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP, device and app information, and ad interaction data.

Where required (for example in the EEA, UK, Switzerland and certain US states), the App asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices (shown when applicable). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

6. Reviews and updates

The App may occasionally show Google Play's In-App Review dialog; your review is handled by Google under your Google account. The App uses Google Play's In-App Updates to tell you when a new version is available.

7. Contacting us

If you write to us, we receive only what you choose to send (your e-mail address and message) and use it only to answer you.

8. Permissions

  • Location (approximate and precise): required by Android to read the WiFi name and scan nearby networks, as described in section 1.
  • Nearby WiFi devices (Android 13+): scan nearby WiFi networks, not used for location.
  • WiFi state and change WiFi state: read signal strength, link speed and start WiFi scans.
  • Internet and network state: speed test, network tools, ads and analytics.
  • Notifications (Android 13+): optional tips and network alerts, only if you enable them.
  • Advertising ID: ads, as described in section 5.

9. Data sharing

We do not sell personal data. We do not collect personal data on our own servers. Data is processed only by the services listed above (Cloudflare for the speed test, Google AdMob and Google Firebase) to provide the features, advertising and app stability.

10. Data retention

Local data stays on your device until you clear it or uninstall the App. Data collected by Google is retained according to Google's policies. E-mails are kept only as long as needed to handle your request.

11. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

12. Security

The App's internet traffic (speed test, ads, analytics) uses encrypted connections (HTTPS).

13. Your rights

Depending on your location you may have rights to access, correct or delete data. For data held by Google, use Google's tools. For any question, contact contact@atdigitalstudio.com.

14. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date shows the latest version.
