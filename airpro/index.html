Privacy Policy — AirPro: Pods Finder

Last updated: November 24, 2026 · Developer: AT Digital Studio · Contact: contact@atdigitalstudio.com

This policy explains how the Android app AirPro: Pods Finder (com.airpro.podsfinder, "the App") handles information.

In short: the App shows the battery and connection state of your Bluetooth earbuds and helps you find nearby earbuds using Bluetooth signal strength. Your Bluetooth data stays on your phone. The App has no account and does not upload your Bluetooth data or your location. The only data sent off your device is the data processed by Google to show ads and run Google Play features, as described below.

1. Bluetooth data

The App reads the names, Bluetooth addresses, connection state and battery levels of your Bluetooth audio devices (from Android and from the battery broadcasts sent by AirPods and Beats), and the signal strength of nearby earbuds when you use the finder. This data is processed and stored only on your phone (recent devices and their last battery reading). It is never sent to us or to anyone else. You can remove a recent device in the App, or delete everything by clearing the App's data.

2. Location

Android requires the location permission for Bluetooth scanning: the battery broadcasts of AirPods and Beats are only delivered to apps that hold it. The App does not use location for tracking, does not use GPS to find your earbuds, and never sends your location to us or to third parties.

The optional "Last seen place" feature (Settings → Features) reads the phone's position, only while the App is in use (no background location), when your earbuds disconnect or while the App is open with them connected, so it can show where they were last connected. Only the latest position is kept, on your phone only; it is not included in backups and never uploaded. Turning the option off deletes it. "Open the map" hands the point to the map app you choose.

3. Notifications and background features

Notifications are used only for the alerts you turn on (low battery, connection changes, case-open card).

The optional "Popup when the case opens" feature (off by default) runs a foreground service of type connectedDevice that monitors the battery broadcast of your earbuds while the feature is on, with an ongoing notification. Optional background updates refresh the battery for widgets and alerts about every 15 minutes. All of this runs on your phone; nothing is uploaded.

4. Data stored on your device

The App stores locally: your recent devices and their last battery readings, the optional last seen place, your settings (theme, language, alerts, chosen earbuds model and styles, unlocked themes), counters used to ask for a rating at most once every 30 days, the date you declined an update, and when ads were last shown (to limit how often they appear). You can delete all of it by clearing the App's data or uninstalling it.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and shows ads through Google AdMob: banners, native ads, full-screen (interstitial) ads, app open ads and optional rewarded ads ("watch an ad to unlock a theme for 24 hours"). Full-screen ads are never shown during a scan, a pairing, a permission request or an update. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process the device advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP, device and app information, and ad interaction data.

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland (and wherever required), the App asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices (shown when applicable). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

6. Purchases

If the App offers the optional Pro version (no ads), purchases are processed by Google Play Billing under your Google account. We receive only the purchase state needed to unlock Pro; we never see your card or bank details. A copy of that state is kept on your device so ads stay off when you are offline.

7. Google Play features and app configuration

The App uses Google Play In-App Review to let you rate the App, and Google Play In-App Updates to tell you when a new version is available and install it. Both are handled by Google Play under your Google account; we do not receive your account details or see who wrote a review.

The App may use Firebase Remote Config (Google) to adjust how often ads may appear. Firebase may process a Firebase installation ID, the app version and device information to deliver this configuration; it is not used for advertising.

8. Permissions

  • Nearby devices (BLUETOOTH_CONNECT, BLUETOOTH_SCAN; BLUETOOTH and BLUETOOTH_ADMIN on Android 11 and earlier): list your paired earbuds, read their battery and connection state, scan for nearby earbuds and pair new ones.
  • Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION): required by Android for Bluetooth scanning; also used by the optional "Last seen place", as described in section 2. No background location.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): only for the alerts you turn on.
  • Foreground service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_CONNECTED_DEVICE): the optional case-open popup.
  • Display over other apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW): optional floating battery card when the case opens, granted by you in system settings.
  • Internet and network state (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): ads, consent form, app configuration and Google Play features.
  • Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID): used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK, as described in section 5.
  • Billing (com.android.vending.BILLING): optional Pro purchase through Google Play.

The App does not request access to your microphone, camera, contacts, photos, files or background location.

9. Data sharing

We do not sell personal data. Bluetooth data and location stay on your phone. Data is processed by Google only to provide advertising, consent management, purchases, app configuration, in-app reviews and in-app updates. We do not use any other analytics, crash reporting or tracking service.

10. Data retention and deletion

The App has no account. Data stored on your device stays there until you delete it in the App, clear the App's data or uninstall it. Data collected by Google is retained according to Google's policies. E-mails you send us are kept only as long as needed to answer you.

11. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

12. Security

Communication with Google services is encrypted (HTTPS). The App does not send your Bluetooth data or your location to any server.

13. Your rights

Depending on your location you may have rights to access, correct or delete data. Because we keep no personal data on our servers, clearing the App's data or uninstalling it erases your data. For data held by Google, use Google's tools. For any question, contact contact@atdigitalstudio.com.
